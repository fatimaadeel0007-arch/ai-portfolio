Case Study 1: Clothing Brand Advertisement

Goal

Create an engaging clothing advertisement that presents affordable, comfortable everyday clothing for university life.

Marketing Message

Affordable, comfortable everyday clothes made for university life — easy-to-wear pieces that look good between classes and on a budget.

Creative Direction

The concept focuses on relatable university life, comfortable clothing, and a simple visual style that connects the product with everyday student needs.

AI-Assisted Process

AI tools were used to support the creative process, including developing the advertising concept, refining the marketing message, and creating visual content.

Result

A complete advertising concept combining a headline, caption, visual direction, and call to action.
