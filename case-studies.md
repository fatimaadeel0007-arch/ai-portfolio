# Case Studies — AI Portfolio for Clothing Brands

## Case Study 1 — AI-Generated Clothing Advertisement

### Audience
Clothing brands

### Challenge
Creating advertising content can involve repetitive work such as developing advertising concepts, writing captions, creating visual directions, and preparing calls to action.

### AI Approach
AI was used to support the development of the advertising message, headline, caption, visual concept, and CTA.

### Main Advertising Message
Affordable, comfortable everyday clothes made for university life — easy-to-wear pieces that look good between classes and on a budget.

### Deliverables
- Main advertising message
- Headline
- Caption
- Visual concept
- Call to action

### Value
The workflow demonstrates how AI can reduce repetitive marketing workload while helping create clear and audience-focused advertising content.

## Case Study 2 — AI Marketing Workflow

### Focus
Building a practical AI-assisted marketing workflow for clothing brands.

### Problem
Marketing work can involve repeated tasks that take time when completed manually.

### Approach
Break the marketing process into clear stages and use AI where it can reduce repetitive workload while keeping human judgment involved.

### Intended Outcome
A practical workflow showing how AI can support clothing-brand marketing without removing human direction and decision-making.

### Status
Portfolio development / build week.
